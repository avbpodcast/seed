How the decks work

Use two standard 52-card decks of the same size. Leave jokers out. A pencil marks twelve extra face cards.

Blue deck — 32 cards

From deck 1 keep only Ace through 8 of every suit. Set the rest aside. The twelve face cards (J Q K of each suit) go to the orange deck later.

Orange deck — 64 cards

Take deck 2 in full. From the leftover face cards of deck 1, mark each Jack, Queen and King with a clear X. A Jack of Hearts and a Jack of Hearts X are two different cards. Add those twelve marked cards to deck 2.

Shuffle and draw

Shuffle each deck on its own. Seven or more riffle shuffles per deck is a reasonable minimum. Blue face-down on the left, orange on the right.

Preferred rhythm: after every pair, return both cards and shuffle again. Repeats are allowed in BIP-39. Faster rhythm: one thorough shuffle, then deal without replacement. Do not do that for 23 entropy words from a 32-card blue deck — you run out of blue cards. Shuffle again if the blue pile is empty.

Each draw is the top blue card plus the top orange card. Enter that pair here, or look it up in the printed booklet. Do this 11 times for a 12-word phrase, or 23 times for 24 words. Those are entropy words only.

The 0-based booklet matches the #index this page prints. The 1-based booklet is the same list with every number raised by one. Do not mix them.

Checksum pair

A 12-word phrase is 128 bits of entropy plus a 4-bit SHA-256 checksum. Eleven pairs give 121 bits, so you still need 7 entropy bits and 4 checksum bits. A 24-word phrase is 256 + 8; twenty-three pairs give 253 bits, so you still need 3 + 8.

Draw one extra pair. This page keeps only the missing entropy bits from that pair and fills the checksum bits from SHA-256. Cards alone cannot compute that hash. The printed last word is the BIP-39 word, not the raw pair word.

If you would rather not use this file for the last word, enter the 11 or 23 entropy words plus a candidate last word on an air-gapped hardware wallet. If it rejects the phrase, discard only the last pair and draw again. Chance of a hit: 1 in 16 for 12 words, 1 in 256 for 24 words.

Do not invent the last word, and do not pick a favourite from the valid endings.
