    Write the words on paper by hand. Never photograph, paste, or retype them on a connected device.
    
  
    The count is one number, repeated. Ranks only (default): 45 for 12 words, 45 then 45 for 24 words. Full cards: 30 for 12 words, 30 then 30 for 24 words. You never mix 45 with 30. Round two is a new shuffle of all 52, not the leftover cards.
    
  
    Round two is a full reshuffle, not a continuation. Every card goes back in, including the ones already face up, and it gets the same shuffle round one got. The tool models each round as a draw from a complete fifty-two card deck, so dealing round two from the leftovers would make the arithmetic wrong and the output non-uniform. This is the one instruction here that cannot be checked in software.
    
  
    The shuffle has more room than it looks. Two full-card rounds of thirty carry 311.3 bits against the 256 needed; two ranks-only rounds of forty-five have a similar surplus. What the ritual does not tolerate is skipping the second shuffle: a round two dealt from an unshuffled deck would repeat round one.
    
  
    The length is fixed before the first card, on purpose. The twelve-word phrase from round one is not merely related to the twenty-four word phrase — it is the first half of it, and shares the first eleven words. If you could generate twelve words, look at them, then extend, anyone who saw that intermediate phrase would hold half your final seed. Changing the length clears the deal.
    
  
    Repeats are caught within a round only. A card already face up in this round cannot come off the deck again, so any repeat is flagged. After the reshuffle that check restarts, since round two can legitimately repeat cards from round one.
    
  
    Ranks-only is the default. One keystroke a card and no suit to read. The round is longer: forty-five ranks instead of thirty full cards. Switch to full cards before the first card if you want the shorter deal.
    
  
    Verify before funding. Restore the phrase on a hardware wallet or a second BIP39 implementation and confirm identical addresses.
    
  
    This is the Basic file. It never derives keys. The Full file adds the first three bech32 addresses, the account zpub, and a SeedQR after the phrase exists.
